# Banned phrases and patterns

Copy is more useful when it says something specific. These add noise; remove
them.

## Banned phrases (do not use)

- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "ever-evolving landscape"
- "delve"
- "leverage"
- "seamless"
- "robust"
- "game-changer"
- "unlock"
- "harness"
- "elevate"
- "It's important to note"
- "When it comes to"
- "In conclusion"

(Not exhaustive: any empty marketing phrasing is banned even if not on this
list.)

## Banned patterns

- Same-shaped paragraphs repeated (every paragraph opening with a pronoun or
  the same clause).
- Every list with exactly three items, especially for effect ("three reasons",
  "three steps") unless the content genuinely has three.
- Excessive bold, emojis, or headings (headings should match real structure,
  not be sprinkled for appearance).
- Hedging everywhere ("might", "could", "arguably") — hedge only where the
  uncertainty is real.
- Generic praise of M91 ("M91 is the best alerting platform"). Praise must be
  earned with a concrete mechanism or a sourced fact.
- Repetition of the same idea in two paragraphs.

## Humanization checklist (from the brief)

A post that passes the checklist:

- contains at least one approved experience-bank item (a real number, story
  or quote);
- teaches something the reader did not know;
- has zero banned phrases/patterns;
- opening sounds like a person talking, not a template;
- takes a clear position somewhere in the piece.
