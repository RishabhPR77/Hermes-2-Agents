# Topic groups

Post topics belong to one of these five groups (used for balance and dupes).

1. **Human-in-the-loop approvals for AI agents** — LangChain, LangGraph,
   Vercel AI SDK, OpenAI Agents SDK, MCP; gating irreversible agent actions.
2. **Fintech and payments alerts and approvals** — payout batches, recovery,
   disputes, UPI, settlement risk, compliance.
3. **On-call alerting and the Opsgenie migration** — Opsgenie shuts down
   5 April 2027 (verify the date in current sources before printing it).
4. **Alerting best practices** — severity, escalation, debouncing, staffing,
   knowing when an alert is worth it.
5. **Comparisons** — M91 vs PagerDuty, Pushover, WhatsApp/Slack groups,
   build-it-yourself.

Balance rule (from config.yaml): no more than two consecutive posts in one
group.

Rapid-response trend briefs and update briefs still land in the group the
topic belongs to.
