# Experience bank

Real stories, numbers and quotes from operating M91. **Only entries marked
"Approved for public: yes" may appear in posts or briefs** (AGENTS.md rule 7).

Mark new entries "Approved for public: no" until the owner explicitly
approves them.

## Format

```
## <Short title>

- **Type:** story | number | quote
- **Approved for public:** yes / no
- **Added:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Detail:** <what happened; the number; the quote and who said it>
```
